# 摘要

风电出力受风速驱动，具有显著的随机性与波动性，其短时间内的功率大幅变化（爬坡事件）是威胁电网安全稳定运行的重要因素。针对现有爬坡预警研究“重预测精度、轻事件级评价”、“输出停留在是/否、缺乏概率语义”、“阈值靠经验设定、缺少代价敏感标定”等不足，本文以 SDWPF 公开风场数据集（134 台机组、201 MW、245 天、10 min 分辨率）为对象，构建了“数据—事件—预测—概率化—决策—系统”一体化的风电功率爬坡预警系统。

全文的主要工作与创新点如下：

（1）**爬坡事件成因归因，为预警能力划清责任边界。** 基于 ΔP = F̄·ΔC + C̄·ΔF 的恒等分解（实测最大残差 2.84×10⁻¹⁴ MW），将爬坡事件分解为工况项与气象项，据此分类为工况型、气象型与混合型，实测气象型占 95.97%。预警指标主要在气象型事件上统计，对工况型事件降级处理，从而解释掉一部分不可避免的漏警，避免模型“背锅”与指标失真。

（2）**零额外训练成本的概率化预警。** 不引入分位数模型，而是复用回归模型在验证集上的残差经验分布，直接构造未来功率的 P10/P50/P90 区间，并以 P(ΔP>θ) = 1 − F_emp(θ − ΔP̂) 计算超阈概率，把预警从“是/否”升级为“以多大概率”。验证集 P10–P90 区间覆盖率 0.8041（目标 0.80），Brier 分数 0.028–0.052，校准良好。

（3）**代价敏感告警决策。** 以 τ* = argmin_τ (C_miss·漏警数 + C_false·虚警数) 标定告警阈值，在测试集上扫描并绘制代价曲线，叠加事件合并与迟滞机制。实验表明代价比改变最优阈值：1:1 带迟滞取 τ*=0.45（归一化代价 0.8140），10:1 取 τ*=0.25；迟滞在 1:1 与 10:1 降低代价，在 5:1 反而上升 0.0233。τ=0.60 及以上在现行概率源下不产生告警，属全漏警退化解，本文如实报告而非作为推荐点。

在算法基础上，本文实现了支持离线回测与实时演示双模式的预警系统：以 LightGBM 为主模型、LSTM/TCN 为对比，二分类“有无爬坡”上 LightGBM 的 AUC 为 0.8643；回归主模型测试集 MAE 11.199 MW、R² 0.8705；在线推理与离线预测偏差 7×10⁻⁶ MW，保证了线上线下同口径。系统采用分层解耦架构，并搭建 Arduino + 传感器 + LED 的实时采集与分级预警子系统，形成“采集—推理—决策—预警”的完整闭环。默认 τ=0.30 + 迟滞下命中率 0.6047、虚警率 0.6941；测试期主口径事件仅 43 条，各档指标存在波动，正文按事件级评价口径如实陈述。

**关键词**：风电功率预测；爬坡事件预警；概率校准；代价敏感决策；事件级评价

# Abstract

Wind power output, driven by wind speed, exhibits pronounced randomness and volatility. Large short-term power fluctuations (ramp events) pose a major threat to the safe and stable operation of power grids. Existing ramp-warning studies suffer from several shortcomings: they prioritize point-forecast accuracy over event-level evaluation, their outputs remain binary (yes/no) without probabilistic semantics, and alarm thresholds are set empirically without cost-sensitive calibration. To address these issues, this thesis builds an integrated wind-power ramp-warning system covering "data–event–forecast–probabilization–decision–system", using the public SDWPF wind-farm dataset (134 turbines, 201 MW, 245 days, 10-minute resolution).

The main contributions and innovations are as follows:

(1) **Causal attribution of ramp events, delineating the responsibility boundary of warning capability.** Based on the algebraic identity ΔP = F̄·ΔC + C̄·ΔF (measured maximum residual 2.84×10⁻¹⁴ MW), each ramp event is decomposed into a working-condition term and a meteorological term, and classified accordingly as working-condition, meteorological, or mixed type; meteorological events account for 95.97% in practice. Warning metrics are mainly computed on meteorological events, while working-condition events are treated separately, thereby explaining part of the unavoidable missed alarms and avoiding distortion of the model's evaluation.

(2) **Probabilistic warning at zero extra training cost.** Instead of introducing a quantile model, we reuse the empirical residual distribution of the regression model on the validation set to construct P10/P50/P90 intervals of future power, and compute the exceedance probability P(ΔP>θ) = 1 − F_emp(θ − ΔP̂) as the output in place of a binary flag. The P10–P90 coverage on the validation set is 0.8041 (target 0.80), with Brier scores of 0.028–0.052, indicating good calibration.

(3) **Cost-sensitive alarm decision.** The alarm threshold is calibrated as τ* = argmin_τ (C_miss·missed + C_false·false alarms). We scan thresholds on the test set to draw cost curves, and combine the decision with event merging and hysteresis. The cost ratio shifts the optimal threshold: τ*=0.45 with hysteresis for 1:1 (normalized cost 0.8140) and τ*=0.25 for 10:1. Hysteresis lowers the cost at 1:1 and 10:1 but raises it by 0.0233 at 5:1. At τ≥0.60 the current probability source triggers no alarm at all, a degenerate all-missed solution that this thesis reports rather than recommends.

Building on these algorithms, a warning system supporting both offline back-testing and real-time demonstration is implemented. LightGBM is the main model (with LSTM/TCN as baselines), achieving an AUC of 0.8643 on the binary "ramp or not" task; the regression model attains MAE 11.199 MW and R² 0.8705 on the test set; the online inference deviates from offline prediction by only 7×10⁻⁶ MW, so the online and offline results agree. The system adopts a layered, decoupled architecture, and an Arduino + sensor + LED real-time acquisition and graded-warning subsystem forms a complete "acquisition–inference–decision–warning" loop. At the default τ=0.30 with hysteresis, the hit rate is 0.6047 and the false-alarm rate is 0.6941 on 43 primary-criterion ramp events in the test set; given this small sample, the event-level metrics fluctuate across thresholds and are reported as measured.

**Keywords**: wind power forecasting; ramp event warning; probability calibration; cost-sensitive decision; event-level evaluation
